Valorferrum is live. This first post explains why I took this step and what the philosophy behind the name is.

## Why Valorferrum?

The name combines *Valor* (value, strength) and *Ferrum* (iron, endurance). This isn't just meant to sound cool – it describes how I work:

- **Value-driven**: Don't sell products that aren't needed.
- **Enduring**: Solutions that work not just today, but in two years as well.

## What to expect

In this blog, I'll write about SEO, SEA, web development and tracking – without buzzwords, without fluff. Practical, technically sound and honest.
